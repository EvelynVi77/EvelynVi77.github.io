Hi, I’m Cheng Zeng (Evelyn)

I’m a Lecturer in Environmental Design at Shenzhen Technology University. I work at the intersection of design, technology, and cognition. With training in architecture and design (M.Arch, Harvard Graduate School of Design; B.Arch & BFA, Rhode Island School of Design) and current teaching in Shenzhen, I explore human–computer interaction, embodied cognition, and AI/XR applications in design, seeking new ways to understand and transform spatial experience.
