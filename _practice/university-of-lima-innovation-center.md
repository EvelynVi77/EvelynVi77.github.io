*Architecture Design, Sasaki Associates, 2018-2019*

Supervisor: Pablo Savid-Butler, Antonio Furukawa. Team Member: Amrit Pilo, Youngjin Lee, Rob Sugar, Yasmin Maura-Orihuela, Evelyn Cheng Zeng.   
Role: Actively contributed to overall concept design; prepared presentation and documentation drawing sets in multiple phases; in charge of structural coordination and involved in MEP coordination.
