Collaborative Design Competition Entry | 2021

*Honorable Mention, Non-Architecture Unbuilt Project Award 2022*

<img src="/assets/design/trace-s/cover.jpg" alt="image" loading="lazy" width="2271" height="1276">

The 416 Memorial Plaza is conceived as a linear shallow water field where each line bears the name of one of the 304 victims of the Sewol Ferry disaster. These water lines interlace anonymously yet reveal a converging directional pattern from above—an embodiment of how individual courage and shared faith unite into collective strength.

<img src="/assets/design/trace-s/image-01.jpg" alt="image" loading="lazy" width="2523" height="1417">

As visitors walk across the plaza, their footprints momentarily imprint upon the shallow water, drying over time. This simple interaction transforms loss into a tangible act of remembrance—each trace joining others to form a shared memory field, one that grows stronger in absence.

<img src="/assets/design/trace-s/image-02.jpg" alt="image" loading="lazy" width="2400" height="1350">

Below, an underground space is shaped by light and water descending from the plaza above. A spatial axis connects Danwon High School and the ferry’s sinking site, guiding visitors through zones of exhibition, reflection, and commemoration. Here, converging streams fall upon a dark reflective surface—becoming a “blackboard” on which traces of memory continue to be inscribed.

- Collaborated with Qinrong Liu, Yuting Zhang, Zishen Wen and Qiaoqi Dai.
