*Featured in the Shenzhen & Hong Kong Bi-City Biennale of Urbanism/Architecture (UABB), Off-World Theater*

[Read the Off-World Theater introduction ↗](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/t0sY--1KfTnxazQlLLnimg){: target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" }

Asteroid-Linked Nexus is a speculative spatial-dwelling concept imagining human habitation anchored to an asteroid-scale celestial body. A network of suspended fragments and faceted structures frames dwelling as an adaptive constellation rather than a fixed enclosure.
