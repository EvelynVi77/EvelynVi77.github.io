Shenzhen Technology University, School of Design and Innovation | New Media Design Required Course | Instructors: Cheng Zeng, Meikang Li | Fall 2025

This course centers on TouchDesigner’s node-based programming. Through a combination of technical instruction and creative practice, it guides students to develop interactive art-making skills grounded in 2D imagery, 3D models, video, and hardware control.

Emphasizing the integration of computational logic and visual expression, the course helps students build dynamic visual works in a systematic way, and lays a solid technical and creative foundation for future exploration in fields such as media playback/control, interaction design, and digital art.
